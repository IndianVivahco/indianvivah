SHYAM SANGAM PARICHAY LLP

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how SHYAM SANGAM PARICHAY LLP may collect, use, display, share and safeguard information in connection with its matrimonial services.

1. Information We May Collect

During registration, profile creation and the provision of matrimonial services, we may collect the following information:

  • Full name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address
  • Date of birth and age
  • Gender
  • Religion, community and related profile information
  • Marital status
  • Education
  • Occupation and employment details
  • Income-related profile information
  • State, district, city and address-related information
  • Height, dietary preferences and lifestyle information
  • Profile photographs
  • Membership and payment-verification details

2. How We Use Information

We may use the information collected for the following purposes:

  • Creating and maintaining member profiles
  • Displaying and helping members discover matrimonial profiles
  • Identifying potential matches and providing human-assisted matrimonial services
  • Providing membership-related services and features
  • Communicating about accounts, enquiries and services
  • Verifying payments
  • Detecting fraud, misuse and security concerns
  • Maintaining and improving website and app functionality

3. Public Profile Information

Certain matrimonial profile details may be displayed to other eligible users for matchmaking purposes. Public visibility may depend on the member’s profile settings, photo-privacy preferences, account status and the features available on the platform. Access to contact details and other sensitive information may be restricted by privacy settings, membership rules and service controls. Sharing private contact information for assisted introductions is subject to the relevant person’s consent.

4. Profile Photographs

Photographs uploaded by members may be used to display their matrimonial profiles. Photo visibility may depend on the selected privacy settings and the platform’s available functionality. A privacy setting does not guarantee that another person cannot capture or retain content they are permitted to view.

5. Identity Documents

If Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID or another identity document is requested for a legitimate verification purpose, it should not be displayed on a public matrimonial profile. We seek to limit the collection and disclosure of such documents to what is necessary for the stated purpose. Members should exercise caution before sending identity documents to other users.

6. Firebase and Cloud Services

SHYAM SANGAM PARICHAY LLP may use third-party cloud services to operate its website and app. Firebase services may support account authentication and profile-data storage, while cloud image services may be used to host profile photographs. Processing by these service providers may also be subject to their applicable privacy and technical safeguards.

7. Payment Information

Members should never enter or share their card PIN, CVV, OTP or internet-banking password on this matrimonial website or with our staff. For UPI or other payments, we may request a transaction reference, UTR, payment screenshot or confirmation solely for verification and record-keeping.

8. Information Sharing

Relevant matrimonial profile information may be made available to eligible users as part of the service, subject to applicable privacy settings and consent. We do not intend to sell personal data or share it for unrelated commercial purposes. We may disclose information when required by applicable law, a lawful request from an authorised authority, or where reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, security incidents or misuse.

9. Account and Profile Updates

Signed-in members may update available profile information through the Edit Profile feature. Certain details may not be directly editable for operational or security reasons. Members may contact the official support channel for assistance with corrections.

10. Data Security

We seek to apply reasonable technical and operational safeguards against unauthorised access, misuse and accidental disclosure of member information. However, no internet-based or cloud system can guarantee absolute security.

11. Member Responsibilities

Members should take the following precautions:

  • Keep login credentials secure
  • Never share OTPs, passwords or financial credentials
  • Report suspicious profiles, messages or behaviour
  • Independently verify identity, marital status and other important facts before making a marriage decision

12. Profile Removal and Deactivation

Members may request that their account or profile be hidden, deactivated or removed through the available official support channel. Certain records may need to be retained for legitimate legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution or technical-backup purposes, as applicable. Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable law and operational requirements.

13. Adults Only

Our matrimonial services are intended for adults who are legally eligible to marry. Matrimonial profile registration for minors is not permitted.

14. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to website or app features, service processes or applicable legal requirements. The updated version will be made available on this page.

15. Contact

For questions about privacy, profile corrections, account access or other data-related concerns, please contact SHYAM SANGAM PARICHAY LLP through its official support channel listed on the website.

For your privacy and safety, do not rely solely on information in a matrimonial profile. Independently verify identity, marital status, employment, family details and other material facts before making decisions.
