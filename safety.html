Safety Guidelines

Before proceeding with a matrimonial connection, prioritise your identity, financial security and personal safety.

1. Independently Verify Profiles

A profile appearing on our website or app does not mean that every detail has been independently verified.

  • Verify the person’s name and identity.
  • Confirm age and marital status.
  • Check education and employment claims.
  • Independently confirm family details.
  • Cross-check the stated city and address.

2. Avoid Rushed Financial Transactions

Requests for loans, emergency expenses, travel tickets, medical bills, investments, gifts or other payments during matrimonial conversations may be warning signs.

Do not transfer money to an unknown person solely on the basis of a matrimonial conversation.

3. Never Share OTPs, UPI PINs or Banking Credentials

  • Never disclose an OTP or UPI PIN.
  • Do not share ATM PINs, card CVVs or online banking passwords.
  • Avoid installing screen-sharing or remote-access apps at another person’s request.

You do not need to enter your UPI PIN to receive a payment.

4. Watch for Suspicious Payment Requests

Verify any request to scan a QR code, approve a UPI collect request, pay a security deposit or send money as a registration charge.

Pay membership fees only through the official payment details displayed on the website or app of SHYAM SANGAM PARICHAY LLP. Verify the recipient details before authorising payment.

5. Arrange First Meetings Safely

  • Meet in a public place.
  • Inform a family member or trusted person.
  • Share the meeting location and time with someone you trust.
  • Avoid isolated locations for a first meeting.
  • Arrange your own transport and return journey.

6. Share Personal Documents Carefully

Do not immediately share Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, bank statements, salary slips or other sensitive documents with an unfamiliar profile.

Where a document is genuinely required for verification, consider masking information that is not necessary for that purpose.

7. Prevent Misuse of Photos and Personal Information

Do not circulate another member’s profile photograph, mobile number, address or personal information on social media, WhatsApp groups or other platforms without appropriate permission and a legitimate purpose.

8. Recognise Potentially Fake or Misleading Profiles

Exercise additional caution when you notice:

  • Frequent changes to profile information.
  • Repeated avoidance of video calls.
  • Reluctance to involve family members.
  • Pressure to marry or commit unusually quickly.
  • Repeated requests for money.
  • Inconsistent statements about employment or location.

9. Use Video Calls and Family Interaction

At an appropriate stage, video calls, family discussions and independent background checks may help establish confidence.

Do not make a final matrimonial decision based only on text messages or profile photographs.

10. Protect Your Account Password

  • Use a strong, unique password.
  • Never share your password.
  • Sign out of shared devices.
  • Do not enter login credentials through suspicious links.

11. Respond to Harassment or Abuse

If anyone engages in harassment, threats, abusive language, stalking, sexual misconduct, blackmail or coercion, stop communicating with that person and report the conduct through the available support or reporting channel. Contact the appropriate authorities where necessary.

12. Act Promptly in Cases of Cyber Fraud

If you suspect financial fraud, account compromise or online cheating, preserve transaction records, screenshots, chats and other relevant evidence.

Contact your bank or UPI provider promptly and report the incident to the appropriate cybercrime authority. In India, you may use the national cybercrime helpline 1930 or the official cybercrime reporting portal.

13. Complete Important Checks Before Marriage

Before making a final marriage decision, independently verify relevant information as appropriate to your circumstances:

  • Identity and age
  • Marital status
  • Family background
  • Employment or business
  • Education
  • Residence
  • Significant financial claims

14. Report Suspicious Activity

Report suspected fake profiles, fraud attempts, abusive conduct, misleading information or suspicious payment requests through the official support channel of SHYAM SANGAM PARICHAY LLP. Keep supporting evidence where available.

15. Final Decisions Belong to Individuals and Families

SHYAM SANGAM PARICHAY LLP assists with matrimonial profile discovery and introductions.

The decision to communicate, meet, become engaged or marry belongs to the individuals concerned, with family involvement where they choose.

Do not rely solely on online profile information. Independent verification of important facts and appropriate family-level due diligence are essential to a safer matrimonial process.
