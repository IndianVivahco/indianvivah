Matrimony Blog & Tips

Rishto ki samaj kaise badhayein?

Ek acche jeevansathi ka chunav karte samay kin baaton ka dhyan rakhna chahiye...

1. Aapsi Samaj: Dono ke vichar aur jeevan ke lakshya milne chahiye.
2. Aadar aur Samman: Ek dusre ki bhavnaon aur pariwar ka samman jaroori hai.
3. Achhi Baatchit: Apni baat khulkar kehne aur dusre ki baat sunne ki aadat.
4. Bharosa: Kisi bhi majboot rishte ki neev bharosa hi hoti hai.
5. Aarthik Soch: Paise ke kharch aur bachat ko lekar dono ke vichar milne chahiye.
6. Pariwar ka Swabhav: Ek dusre ke parivar ke rehan-sehan aur sanskaron ko samjhein.
7. Gusse par Kabu: Gusse mein galat shabd na bolne wale sathi ka chunav karein.
8. Aatmanirbharta: Sathi ka dimagi aur aarthik roop se majboot hona jaroori hai.
9. Badlav ki Taiyari: Shadi ke baad aane wale badlavon ko apnane ki samajh honi chahiye.
10. Pasand-Napasand: Ek dusre ki khushi aur pasand ka dhyan rakhna jaroori hai.

Vivahik jeevan ko khushhal kaise banayein?

Shadi ke baad aapsi talmel aur bharosa banane ke aasan tarike...

1. Waqt bitayein: Rozana ek dusre ke liye thoda samay jaroor nikalein.
2. Galtiyan maaf karein: Purani baaton ko pakad kar na baithein, maaf karna seekhein.
3. Ek dusre ki madad: Ghar ke kaamon aur zimmedariyon mein ek dusre ka haath batayein.
4. Chhoti khushiyan: Ek dusre ko bina kisi moke ke chhote surprises ya tohfe dein.
5. Pariwar ko Samman: Ek dusre ke mata-pita aur parivar ko pura aadar dein.
6. Milkar Faisle: Ghar ka koi bhi bada faisla dono milkar aapsi salah se karein.
7. Purani Baatein Bhulein: Beete hue kal ki jhagdo wali baaton ko baar-baar beech mein na layein.
8. Prashansa Karein: Ek dusre ke achhe kaamon ki taarif karna kabhi na bhoolein.
9. Space Dein: Rishte mein thodi niji aazadi (personal space) dena bhi jaroori hai.
10. Sehat ka Dhyan: Ek dusre ki mansik aur sharirik sehat ka poora khyal rakhein.
