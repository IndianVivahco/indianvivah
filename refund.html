Refund Policy

Please read this Refund and Cancellation Policy carefully before purchasing a matrimonial membership.

1. Membership Plans

SHYAM SANGAM PARICHAY LLP offers the following matrimonial introduction plans, subject to the features and terms shown at the time of purchase:

Silver₹3,500Up to 3 months · 12 matrimonial proposals
Gold₹6,000Up to 6 months · 25 matrimonial proposals
Platinum₹10,000Up to 12 months · 50 matrimonial proposals

Each plan includes phone-assisted matrimonial introduction and coordination. Service concludes when the specified number of proposals has been provided or the plan validity expires, whichever occurs first. Proposals may be provided before the end of the validity period.

The final price, scope and terms disclosed and accepted at purchase will apply, subject to applicable law.

2. Payment Verification and Activation

Payments may be verified before a membership is activated. We may request a transaction reference, UTR, payment confirmation or screenshot to verify payment.

Membership activation is subject to successful verification and confirmation. A payment screenshot alone does not establish receipt of funds.

3. Cancellation Before Activation

If a verified payment has been received but membership activation and service delivery have not started, the customer may request cancellation promptly through the official support channel. The request will be reviewed in light of the payment status, agreed terms and applicable consumer law.

Where work has already commenced with the customer's authorization, the services actually provided may be considered when assessing any lawful deduction or refund.

4. Cancellation After Activation

Once a membership has been activated and matrimonial introduction or coordination services have begun, a refund is not automatically due solely because:

  • The customer did not prefer a particular proposed match.
  • A prospective match or family declined to proceed.
  • A marriage was not finalized.
  • The customer stopped participating or did not use the available service.
We provide matrimonial proposals and related assistance, not a guarantee of marriage. However, this provision does not exclude refunds or other remedies for non-delivery, material service deficiencies, misrepresentation or rights available under applicable law.

5. Duplicate Payments

If the same membership is paid for more than once by mistake, the additional verified payment will be reviewed for refund or adjustment after transaction reconciliation.

6. Failed or Incorrect Transactions

If money is debited from the customer's account but is not received by us, the customer should first check the transaction status with the relevant bank or UPI service provider. Only a successfully received and verified transaction will be treated as a membership payment.

7. Payments to an Incorrect Recipient

Before approving a payment, customers should check the recipient's name, UPI ID and amount. A transfer independently made to an incorrect recipient or UPI ID is not treated as a payment received by us. Customers should promptly raise such transactions with their bank or UPI provider.

8. Inability to Provide the Agreed Service

If we receive verified payment but cannot activate the purchased membership or provide the agreed service, we will review the circumstances and offer an appropriate refund, adjustment or other lawful resolution. Any mandatory consumer remedies remain available.

9. How to Request a Refund

For a payment or refund review, contact the official support channel and provide, where available:

  • Registered name, mobile number and email address
  • Selected membership plan and amount paid
  • Payment date and UTR or transaction ID
  • Payment confirmation or screenshot
  • A clear explanation of the request

Do not send your UPI PIN, OTP, card CVV or banking password.

10. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will ordinarily be processed to the original payment source, or through another mutually verified method where appropriate. The time required for funds to appear may depend on the bank, UPI provider or payment network. Customers will be informed of the outcome through the available support channel.

11. Fraud or Misuse

Fraudulent payment claims, altered screenshots, false transaction references or misuse of an account may result in additional verification, rejection of an unsupported claim or proportionate account restrictions, subject to applicable law. Genuine consumer complaints will still be reviewed fairly.

12. Applicable Consumer Rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to waive, limit or override mandatory rights or remedies available to customers under applicable consumer protection or other laws. Where the law requires a different outcome, the law will prevail.

13. Contact

For membership activation, payment verification, cancellation or refund assistance, please use the official support channel listed on our website. Include the relevant transaction details so the request can be reviewed.

Before making a payment, review the plan price, validity, proposal count and included services. Never disclose your OTP, UPI PIN, card CVV or banking password.
